    Set up the extension (once)
    1. In Chrome or Edge, open the extensions page. The coach can’t open it for you: copy this into the address bar. chrome://extensions
    2. Turn on Developer mode (a switch at the top right in Chrome, on the left in Edge).
    3. Press Load unpacked and choose this folder: …
    4. Open any video on youtube.com. A small 𝄞 Music Hub box appears at its top right, and the light above turns green.

    When the extension’s files change, press its reload button (↻) on the extensions page, then reload the YouTube tab.

            Connections

            Keyboard missing? Plug it in and press Refresh. Only one program can use it at a time, so close other piano apps first.

            Microphone & speakers

            Plug in a USB microphone or headset at any time: it's added to the list straight away. Speak and watch the bar move to check it hears you.

            Windows voices always use the Windows default speakers. The keyboard's own sound comes from Sound out, under Connections.

            Audio to MIDI

            How the microphone turns the flute, sax or voice into notes.
