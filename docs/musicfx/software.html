Visualizer · Web

MusicFX Visualizer

A full-screen, real-time note highway that runs in Chrome or Edge. No install, no account.

  • Freeplay: plug in and play, no file needed
  • Learn Mode: load MIDI or MusicXML, choose tracks and hands, and play along in Wait or Free-play mode
  • Effects: glow, sparks, smoke, WebGL fluid ink and GLSL shader notes
  • Themes & scenes: save full looks and switch them per song
  • Hardware: map MIDI CCs to any control and stream to your MusicFX Hub over USB or Bluetooth

App · Desktop

MusicFX Practice Coach

A practice companion for Claude Desktop that listens to you play and tells you what to work on.

  • Records your playing and compares it with the score
  • Flags wrong notes, timing, tempo, dynamics and pedal, bar by bar
  • Tracks your progress over time
  • Plays passages back on your keyboard and sends custom lessons to Learn Mode

Firmware · Hub

Open Hub firmware

The MusicFX Hub ships ready to use. The firmware is open, so you can update it or adapt it to your own builds.

  • Receives frames over USB serial or Bluetooth LE
  • Drives a per-key strip and a mirrored ambient strip at the same time
  • Simple text frame protocol, documented in the repository

Audience colour voting

Viewers vote in chat on themes, colours, effects and song presets. The winner changes the visualizer and your lights live.

OBS overlay

A transparent browser source that shows the current poll and slides away between rounds.

Ready-made song presets, plus prompts that let any AI assistant write a new theme for you.
