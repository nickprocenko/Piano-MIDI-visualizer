Size guide

Which size do I need?

Each light is sized to your instrument. Strips can be trimmed, so if you're between sizes, choose the larger one.

Keys: count the keys on your keyboard

Lengths are for full-size keys. For mini keys, choose by key count and trim the extra strip.

Drums: measure each drum's head diameter

One DrumRing per drum. Using an electronic kit? Skip the Hit Sensors, because the Drum Hub reads your module's MIDI.

Guitar: count the frets on your neck

The FretStrip runs along the top (player-facing) edge of the fretboard, with a light at every fret.
