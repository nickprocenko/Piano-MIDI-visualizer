How it works

Up and running in three steps

STEP 01

Fit the lights

Put the LightStrip above your keys, clip DrumRings inside your shells or stick the FretStrip along your neck. No drilling, and everything comes off cleanly.

STEP 02

Connect the Hub

Plug the lights into your instrument's Hub, then connect it to your computer with USB-C or pair it over Bluetooth.

STEP 03

Open MusicFX and play

Launch the MusicFX app in Chrome or Edge, pick your instrument and Hub, and every note and hit lights up.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which instruments does MusicFX support?

Keyboards, drum kits and guitars (including acoustic and bass). Each has its own lights and Hub, and all of them run from the same MusicFX app.

Will it work with my keyboard?

If your keyboard sends MIDI over USB (or through a USB-MIDI adapter), yes. LightStrips come in 25, 49, 61, 76 and 88-key sizes. See the size guide for measurements.

Do DrumRings work with acoustic drums? Do they change the sound?

Yes, they work with acoustic and electronic kits. On acoustic drums, a clip-on Hit Sensor tells the Drum Hub when each drum is hit. On electronic kits, the Drum Hub reads your module's MIDI instead. The rings clip lightly inside the shell, away from the heads, so they don't change how the drum sounds.

Where does the FretStrip go, and will it get in the way?

It sits on the top (player-facing) edge of the fretboard, where side dots usually are. It's very thin, never touches the strings or the playing surface, and uses a finish-safe adhesive.

How does MusicFX know what I'm playing on guitar or drums?

On drums, through Hit Sensors or your e-kit's MIDI. On guitar, the MusicFX app listens through your audio interface or a MIDI guitar pickup. In Learn Mode it lights up the notes to play next.

Do I need a computer?

Keys and guitar lights are driven by the MusicFX app on your computer, over USB-C or Bluetooth. The Drum Hub can also light your drums on its own, with no computer. The upcoming Hub Pro will do the same for keys.

Which browsers does the app support?

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers, which support Web MIDI. Firefox and Safari don't yet.

Can I buy the parts separately?

Yes. Every light, controller and accessory is sold on its own. The Complete Kits bundle them at a discount.

Will the mounts damage my instrument?

No. Nothing needs drilling. LightStrips and FretStrips use removable adhesive that comes off cleanly, and DrumRings use clips. The DrumRing cable exits through the drum's air vent.

Is the software really free?

Yes. The MusicFX app, Practice Coach and Hub firmware are free and open source.
