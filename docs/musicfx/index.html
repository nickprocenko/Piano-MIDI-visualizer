How it works

Up and running in three steps

STEP 01

Mount the LightStrip

Stick the strip above your keys with the included removable mounts. Each key gets two LEDs.

STEP 02

Connect the Hub

Plug the strip into the Hub, then connect the Hub to your computer with USB-C or pair over Bluetooth.

STEP 03

Open the visualizer and play

Launch the MusicFX Visualizer in Chrome or Edge, pick your keyboard and Hub, and every note lights up.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Will it work with my keyboard?

If your keyboard sends MIDI over USB (or through a USB-MIDI adapter), yes. LightStrips come in 25, 49, 61, 76 and 88-key sizes. See the size guide for measurements.

Do I need a computer?

With the MusicFX Hub, the visualizer runs on your computer and sends the light data over USB-C or Bluetooth. The upcoming Hub Pro plugs into your keyboard directly and works without a computer.

Which browsers does the visualizer support?

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers, which support Web MIDI. Firefox and Safari don't yet.

Can I buy the parts separately?

Yes. Every strip, controller and accessory is sold on its own. The Complete and Stage Kits bundle them at a discount.

Will the mounts damage my keyboard?

No. The mounts use removable adhesive designed to come off cleanly, and the strip sits above the keys without touching them.

Is the software really free?

Yes. The visualizer, Practice Coach and Hub firmware are free and open source.
